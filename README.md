# Wedding Invitation — Aparna & Karthik (Video Edition)

An elegant, mobile-first wedding celebration invitation with an interactive video player, 8-photo portrait gallery, and bespoke monogram cover unfold.

## Features
- **Interactive Video Player**:
  - Transparent central play watermark (fades out on play, reappears on pause).
  - YouTube-style controls: timeline seek/scrub bar, play/pause, time tracker, and volume slider.
  - Native fullscreen button disabled for a seamless inline presentation.
  - Plays with full audio when tapped/clicked.
- **8-Photo Portrait Gallery**: Seamless 3D touch carousel slider.
- **Royal Monogram Cover**: Smooth scroll-to-unfold opening page.
- **Event & Venue Details**: Ceremony & Reception timings with Google Maps venue navigation.
